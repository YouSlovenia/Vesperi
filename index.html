VESPERI

Phonology 🌙

Consonants

/ʃ/ sh, /ʒ/ zh, /l/, /ɾ/ tapped r, /m/, /n/

Vowels

/ɛ/ (as in "bed"), /ɔ/ (as in "thought"), /aː/ (long "ah")

Phonotactics

Syllables: (C)V(V) only. Stress: penultimate syllable.
Example: "A'lira" (ah-LEE-rah) - "moon reflection"

Grammar ✨

Verb Conjugation (Moon Phases)

Waning (past): -eth suffix
Full (present): -ara suffix
Waxing (future): -ine suffix

"Lethara" (I listen now), "Lethine" (I will listen)

Noun Classes

Luminescent (-il): Objects touched by moonlight
Shadowed (-ul): Darkness-bound entities

Lexicon 📜

Vire (n.): The silence between owl calls before dawn

Es'tara (phrase): "We walk unseen" (lit. unseen-path walk-we)

Karil (n.): Moonflower (luminescent class)
